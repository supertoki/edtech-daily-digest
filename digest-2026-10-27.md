# EdTech Daily Digest — October 27, 2026

## Executive Summary

EdSurge and Inside Higher Ed both landed on the same point today: **start from how students learn**. The University of Maryland trial (about 15% usage) and an employer survey showing degrees gaining value keep the focus on proof. EdTech Magazine's human-centered IT piece, Ed-Fi's statewide data stories and 1EdTech's November webinars round out the people and data side. ## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Value & Evidence.** Trials and surveys test **whether AI delivers**: low usage at Maryland, and employers still valuing degrees.

**2. AI Policy & Guardrails.** Writers push back on **bans and pauses**, arguing students need AI skills and civic judgment built first.

**3. Security & Privacy.** Training alone does not stop phishing, and **student data consent** and cyber readiness stay open questions.

**4. People & Skills.** Human-centered IT support and **learning-science-first** faculty decisions put people ahead of tools.

**5. Data & Interoperability.** **Ed-Fi** states report big savings and real-time dashboards; 1EdTech focuses on learn-to-work data.

**6. Platforms & Sovereignty.** Colleges weigh **AI-agnostic, self-built platforms** against dependence on large vendors.
