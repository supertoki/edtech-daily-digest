## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Policy & Guardrails.** Districts and states are still writing AI rules: **over 30 states** have guidance, but few districts have district-level policy.

**2. Privacy & Security.** CoSN finds **cybersecurity and data privacy** remain top AI concerns, with shadow AI an emerging K-12 risk.

**3. AI Value & Evidence.** Brookings finds AI helps when **narrow and well designed**, while general-purpose use can erode learning.

**4. Faculty, Teachers & Students.** Faculty skepticism, student ambivalence and a push for the **right to refuse AI** shape campus adoption.

**5. Data & Interoperability.** 1EdTech and Ed-Fi work on **standards and shared data** that let systems and states connect.

**6. Events & Convenings.** EdTech Week NYC and the 1EdTech Technical Congress anchor a busy October calendar.
