Friday's edtech coverage skews toward analysis over headlines. EdTech Magazine's K-12 team profiles **AI-powered classroom observation tools** and a new joint procurement guide meant to help districts buy technology more rigorously, while a companion piece urges IT leaders to treat **vendor risk management** as core cyber hygiene after this year's Canvas and PowerSchool breaches.
