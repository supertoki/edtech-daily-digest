# EdTech Daily Digest — November 19, 2026

## Executive Summary

Thursday's digest leads with **enrollment comebacks**. Inside Higher Ed reports that the National Student Clearinghouse counted **more than one million stop-outs re-enrolling in 2024–25**, up 3.4 percent, and that Common App international applications for 2026–27 are up **9 percent** even as overall international enrollment fell 9 percent this fall; a Kentucky fight over race- and sex-restricted donor scholarships adds a legal wrinkle. In classrooms, the AI conversation is splitting: EdSurge features a proposal to **grade the chat, not just the answer**, alongside a literacy teacher explaining why he keeps AI out of his room, while THE Journal argues AI's value should be judged by what it preserves for educators, not only the time it saves. EdTech Magazine profiles Wichita's grade-banded approach to **AI that enriches learning**, and EdTech Digest urges districts to move from procurement-led to **outcome-led buying**. On the data front, 1EdTech hosts a Learn-to-Work Ecosystem webinar today as its Member Assembly wraps, and Ed-Fi's API v8.1 and fall roadmap set up Data Standard 7.0 for 2027. EDUCAUSE continues in Orlando through Friday.
