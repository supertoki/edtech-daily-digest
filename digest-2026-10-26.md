# EdTech Daily Digest — October 26, 2026

Meanwhile **tech sovereignty**, AI policy guidance, and Ed-Fi's statewide data stories show institutions seeking control of their stacks and data. Coverage dated today was thin; several sources are represented by their most recent items.

## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Value & Evidence.** Leaders ask **where AI adds real value**, and trials and vendor reviews show adoption outrunning proof.

**2. AI Policy & Guardrails.** Districts, states and cities are **pausing, guiding or requiring** AI literacy as policies mature.

**3. Security & Privacy.** Attackers exploit **trusted workplace tools** and agentic AI raises identity risk, while child-data questions persist.

**4. People & Skills.** Teachers and provosts use AI personally but **training and strategy lag**; colleges add AI fluency requirements.

**5. Data & Interoperability.** States using **Ed-Fi** and 1EdTech standards report big savings and faster, real-time reporting.

**6. Platforms & Sovereignty.** Institutions weigh **AI-agnostic platforms** and open infrastructure against dependence on Big Tech.
