# EdTech Daily Digest — November 17, 2026

## Executive Summary

Tuesday's digest centers on a **trust gap** around AI. An Inside Higher Ed Student Voice survey of 5,034 undergraduates finds eight in ten use generative or agentic AI routinely and **a quarter have changed how they write to avoid false AI-detector flags**, while 88% of surveyed faculty say AI has hurt their confidence in the authenticity of student work. On the K-12 side, CoSN delivered a petition from **464 leaders in 35 states** urging Congress to protect E-Rate as the FCC reviews the program, and EdTech Magazine explains how isolated recovery environments help districts bounce back from ransomware. 1EdTech's new AI-evaluation white paper and EdTech Digest's "evidence season" column keep efficacy in focus, and the fall conference circuit (OLC Accelerate, CITE, BrainStorm) is in full swing.
