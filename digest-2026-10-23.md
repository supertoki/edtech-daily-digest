# EdTech Daily Digest — October 23, 2026

## Executive Summary

Today's thread is **control and caution**. Inside Higher Ed's Oct. 22 column asks whether **tech sovereignty** can free campuses from Big Tech, citing Syracuse's AI-agnostic platform that reportedly kept AI costs 85% below alternatives, while a Maryland randomized trial found an AI study assistant marginally **reduced final grades** and only ~15% of students used it. On the product side, EdTech Magazine reports Google's new Classroom-centered Gemini tools, and THE Journal covers OpenAI retiring Custom GPTs for a plugin model and a report that ~30% of monitored organizations saw **Teams IT-impersonation attempts**. Brookings and ISTE keep the student-protection and teacher-training agenda in view, and 1EdTech's 16-organization learning-mobility alliance advances shared standards.
