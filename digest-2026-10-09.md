# EdTech Daily Digest — October 9, 2026

## Executive Summary

EdReports found that only **one of 10 edtech providers** cited third-party evidence of student outcomes for its AI features, even as nearly all call them effective. In higher ed, Inside Higher Ed's provost survey shows about **7 in 10 provosts use AI weekly but only 1 in 10 report a centralized institutional AI strategy**. Meanwhile early-childhood educators are pushing back on AI-generated video, Brookings keeps framing AI as helpful only when narrowly and carefully designed, and EdTech Week (NYC) and UPCEA Convergence arrive next week.

## Key Themes & Trends

**1. Outcomes & Evidence.** EdReports finds **scant third-party evidence** behind AI product claims; buyers are asked to demand proof.

**2. AI Backlash & Guardrails.** Educators push back on low-quality AI video for young children while Brookings urges **narrow, well-designed use**.

**3. Higher Ed AI Strategy.** Provosts use AI constantly but **few institutions have a central strategy**; faculty remain wary.

**4. Infrastructure & Data.** Interoperability bodies such as Ed-Fi and 1EdTech continue to anchor district data and tool integration.

**5. Teacher AI Adoption.** Big tech keeps launching teacher-facing AI tools, and teachers are increasingly leading integration.

**6. Fall Events Ahead.** **UPCEA Convergence (Oct 13–15)** and **EdTech Week NYC** kick off next week.
