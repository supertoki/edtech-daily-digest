# EdTech Daily Digest — October 19, 2026

## Executive Summary

This week's coverage converges on one question: **where does AI add real value?** Educause's 2027 Top 10 ranks it first for the first time, provosts use AI personally but few institutions have a strategy, and a University of Maryland trial of its own study assistant was inconclusive. In K-12, the Chan Zuckerberg-backed **Learning Commons** launched an open platform with 70+ partners, while Brookings and EdSurge voices debate caution versus panic. Ed-Fi's state stories and post-ESSER tutoring data show that durable infrastructure and funding choices matter as much as the tools.
