**1. Evidence & AI Evaluation.** 1EdTech's new white paper and an EdTech Digest column push for **measurable efficacy** as federal guidance ties procurement to evidence.

**2. AI Guidance & Governance.** Task forces, age-based matrices and survey data show institutions **formalizing AI policy** while training and guidance still lag use.

**5. Student Voice & Civic Purpose.** Student petitions, AI councils and civics lessons after the midterms put **learner agency** at the center of the AI debate.

**6. Data Standards & Interoperability.** Ed-Fi's fall roadmap and state attendance dashboards show **shared data standards** turning into earlier interventions.
