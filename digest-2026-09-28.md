# EdTech Daily Digest — September 28, 2026

## Executive Summary

THE Journal reports that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is the first broadly deployed model to cross the **"Critical"** cybersecurity threshold under the company's Preparedness Framework, scoring **100% on ExploitBench** and surfacing two new zero-day vulnerabilities in testing -- a capability jump that raises the stakes for campus IT teams already managing a widening device and IoT attack surface, as EdTech Magazine's look at cyber-physical security convergence in higher ed makes clear. Underneath the AI headlines, the data and credentialing infrastructure keeps advancing: Ed-Fi named its 2026 Premier Partners, 1EdTech's CEO argues "together is the new standard" for interoperable, trustworthy edtech, and EdTech Digest covers a wave of investors following workers' skills gaps into the workplace-learning market.
