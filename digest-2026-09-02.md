# EdTech Daily Digest — September 2, 2026

## Executive Summary

As the school year settles in, the sector's governance conversation is moving from position papers to practice. THE Journal and EdSurge both revisit the Department of Education's August 20 guidance on classroom technology and screen time, and THE Journal's coverage of CoSN's new "purpose before minutes" framework — echoed by EdTech Digest's own screen-time piece — shows district leaders converging on a quality-over-quantity standard rather than blanket bans. On the AI side, EdSurge sends a principal and a high schooler to stress-test OpenAI's new ChatGPT for Teens, finding real reservations even as ISTE's AI-literacy partnership with Google keeps rolling out to millions of educators and Brookings' task force continues to urge caution about student cognitive risk. THE Journal also flags a governance shift on the enterprise side, reporting agentic AI is moving from pilots into production and forcing stricter autonomy controls, while Anthropic's new invisible watermarking for Claude-generated text points to a broader push for AI-content transparency. In higher ed, Inside Higher Ed hosts a same-day webcast on scaling apprenticeships, against a backdrop of 61% of colleges hitting fall 2026 enrollment goals and a Texas return-to-campus mandate pushing some faculty out the door — a strain EdTech Magazine ties directly to campus IT budgets. On the standards and events side, EdTech Digest profiles outgoing CoSN chief Keith Krueger as 1EdTech, Ed-Fi, and ISTE build toward a packed fall calendar — Learning Impact Europe, EDUCAUSE, and the Ed-Fi Alliance Summit — while Ed-Fi's next data-standard release adds a PII "flag" built for AI discovery.
