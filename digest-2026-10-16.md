## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Value & Evidence.** New testing finds AI models **do the work but struggle to support learning**, echoing calls to demonstrate impact before scaling.

**2. AI Policy & Backlash.** Australia's AERO urges **sparing AI use** in schools, following NYC's one-year pause on student-facing generative AI for Pre-K to 8.

**3. Student Protection & Trust.** Clinicians want **edtech health checks**, and Microsoft's AFT/UFT-backed privacy standard begins applying to school contracts November 1.

**4. Teachers, Faculty & Students.** Digital Promise's grants of up to $600,000 aim to give **teachers more control over AI** tools.

**5. Funding & Partnerships.** Google.org's $5M for Georgia Tech and Google Cloud's Gemini deal with Arden University show **vendor money still flowing** into AI education.

**6. Fall Events.** EdTech Week 2026 at Teachers College, Columbia wraps up, with an **AI for Educators Summit** following October 20.
