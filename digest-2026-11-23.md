## Key Themes & Trends

**1. Big Tech AI Defaults in Schools.** Google's plan to turn **AI and Search off by default** for student accounts has districts building workarounds and weighing other search engines.

**2. AI in Teaching & Learning.** Educators are moving from banning AI to **assessing how students use it**, with grade-banded expectations, graded chat transcripts and age-based guidance.

**3. Cybersecurity & AI Governance.** **Half of K-12 schools** had a cyber incident last year. AI policies and vendor oversight are still catching up with the features vendors keep adding.

**4. Research Integrity in the AI Era.** Hidden **prompts aimed at AI reviewers** in published papers, and studies that rate AI peer review favorably, are testing academic publishing norms.

**5. Interoperability & Data Standards.** Ed-Fi's **API v8.1** and 2027 roadmap, together with 1EdTech's QTI survey and awards cycle, are setting next year's data-standards agenda.

**6. Higher Ed Policy & Politics.** A **House education committee shake-up** and Florida's push to enforce the federal 'Call to Action' show politics reshaping campus oversight.
