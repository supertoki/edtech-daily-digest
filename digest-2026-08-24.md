# EdTech Daily Digest — August 24, 2026

## Executive Summary

The U.S. Department of Education issued its long-awaited edtech guidance for states and districts, stressing that technology use must be **purposeful, instructionally sound, and tied to measurable outcomes** — but leaving actual rulemaking to states, per EdSurge. Brookings argues policymakers should go further, publishing a case for school-based **student AI councils** that give young people a real role in shaping the rules written for them, a theme echoed by EdSurge's look at what students themselves think about AI policy and EdTech Digest's more nuanced take on screen-time rules. On the operations side, EdTech Magazine reports K-12 districts are shifting from reactive incident response to long-term **cyber resilience strategy**, while Inside Higher Ed finds 42% of colleges still haven't fully submitted required federal student-outcomes data — both pointing to the same underlying data-governance strain that Ed-Fi's interoperability case studies exist to solve. Higher ed IT leaders face a tightening budget squeeze: EdTech Magazine covers the new Workforce Pell Grant's implications for community colleges and a proposed federal shift of research funding away from universities, while Inside Higher Ed reports the Education Department's sweeping new accreditation rule is now open for public comment. Meanwhile 1EdTech and EdTech Week are turning toward fall conference season — a credentialing-data webinar this week, Learning Impact Europe in September, and EdTech Week's October NYC gathering with its invite-only StartEd CEO Summit.
