# EdTech Daily Digest — September 3, 2026

## Executive Summary

Higher ed's fall term is opening under real strain: Inside Higher Ed reports a new international-student rule capping status at four years is already driving disenrollments at flagship universities, and a Texas law forcing faculty back to campus five days a week is prompting resignations — pressures EdTech Magazine ties directly to campus IT budgets, echoing its K-12 companion piece on IT leaders bracing for enrollment declines. On the policy side, EdSurge revisits the Department of Education's August 20 guidance distinguishing "recreational" from "instructional" technology, while ISTE+ASCD's new AI-literacy partnership with Google — aiming to train six million educators — and Brookings' ongoing Global Task Force work push the AI conversation from classroom experimentation toward governance and evaluation. EdTech Digest's own screen-time piece argues the debate there is moving the same direction, past blanket bans toward distinguishing tools that empower learning from those that merely distract. Cybersecurity gets the same shared-responsibility framing: 1EdTech is running a September webinar on LMS security just as Inside Higher Ed finds only 22% of campus CTOs confident their students get adequate security training, with "ghost student" AI-bot fraud already exploiting the gap. Underneath it all, the standards layer keeps consolidating — Ed-Fi's latest release ships a native OneRoster service built to 1EdTech's own rostering spec, a concrete step toward the interoperability 1EdTech's trend-watchers call non-negotiable — while EdTech Digest profiles outgoing CoSN chief Keith Krueger as the fall conference calendar (Learning Impact Europe, ISTE certification cohorts, EdTech Week's return to Columbia, and EDUCAUSE) builds toward a head over the next two months.
