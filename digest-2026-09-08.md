# EdTech Daily Digest — September 8, 2026

## Executive Summary

Ed tech's community marks a changing of the guard this week: EdTech Digest bids farewell to **Keith Krueger** after 32 years leading CoSN, even as ISTE+ASCD names new board leadership heading into its next conference cycle. The transition lands amid mounting evidence that policy is outpacing practice — EdSurge profiles a researcher who spent a year coding AI guidance from more than 100 school districts into a five-level framework, and pairs it with a Gallup/Walton Family Foundation survey finding most teachers have received no formal training on classroom AI at all. Brookings adds an equity lens to the broader screen-time and AI-restriction wave sweeping districts, warning that **uniform enforcement** can fall hardest on students with disabilities and Black students unless policies are calibrated rather than applied as blanket rules. Interoperability keeps expanding outward: 1EdTech heads to Thessaloniki for Learning Impact Europe and a Latin America assessment webinar, Ed-Fi ships API v8.0, and EDUCAUSE's late-September conference will draw much of the same crowd. On the access and accountability side, Common App's direct-admissions program grows to 234 colleges for the 2026-27 cycle just as Inside Higher Ed reports the U.S. Department of Education is still chasing over a thousand institutions for overdue student-outcomes data — a compliance gap EdTech Magazine's new procurement guide implicitly argues better tooling could help close. The fall conference calendar keeps filling in behind it all, from EdTech Week's October return to Columbia to Ed-Fi's November Alliance Summit.
