# EdTech Daily Digest — October 1, 2026

## Executive Summary

EdSurge's latest "This Week" episode asks **whether schools can trust AI features in edtech products**, with EdReports' Courtney Allison on why the line between core and supplemental materials is disappearing. Inside Higher Ed's Instructure-based data finding that **AI integration in higher ed is overhyped** remains the lead higher-ed story, alongside coverage of AI reshaping student success and colleges embedding AI literacy (Agnes Scott, Bryn Mawr, DeVry). ## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Hype vs. Evidence.** Usage data and efficacy questions show **AI adoption outrunning proof** of learning impact.

**2. Student Risk and Protection.** Brookings and EdSurge voices warn that **general-purpose AI can diminish learning** unless guardrails are designed in.

**3. Educator Readiness.** Only **11% of higher-ed instructors** report comprehensive AI training, and most K-12 teachers lack formal guidance.

**4. Data and Standards Infrastructure.** Ed-Fi and 1EdTech standards keep **powering real-time student support** and interoperability.

**5. AI Literacy and Curriculum.** Colleges from Agnes Scott to DeVry are **embedding AI literacy** into required coursework.

**6. Convenings and Community.** **EdTech Week, Tech Congress and ISTE+ASCD** gatherings in October will set the fall agenda.
