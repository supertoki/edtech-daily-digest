# EdTech Daily Digest — October 22, 2026

## Executive Summary

The through-line today is **evidence before adoption**. Inside Higher Ed's Educause 2026 coverage reports that deciding where AI adds real value is the number-one 2027 priority for campus tech leaders, while an Instructure analysis finds the most popular AI tool didn't crack the top 100 LTI tools and only about 11% of instructors have comprehensive AI training. THE Journal and Inside Higher Ed both published opinion pieces arguing that **learning science should guide AI use decisions**, and EdSurge asks whether schools are rewarding polished AI output over curiosity. On the infrastructure side, **1EdTech** announced a 16-organization learner-mobility alliance after its Technical Congress and is retiring LTI 1.1 by October 2027, Ed-Fi's v6.1 standard adds a special-education data domain, and security reporting shows attackers impersonating IT staff on Teams.
