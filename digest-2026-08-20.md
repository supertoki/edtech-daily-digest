# EdTech Daily Digest — August 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

Thursday's news is about consequences. UT San Antonio **delayed the start of classes by three days** after detecting unauthorized activity against its systems, a live reminder of why K-12 and higher-ed IT teams are shifting from reactive incident response to long-term cyber resilience. Meanwhile the AI conversation turned economic: EdSurge asks **who actually pays** for an AI-first classroom, higher-ed IT budgets absorb a federal research-funding realignment, and campus fraud teams battle AI-generated "ghost students" siphoning financial aid. Governance is catching up from the bottom, with Brookings making the case for **student-led AI councils** and the Education Department opening a proposed accreditation rule for public comment. On the standards side, 1EdTech closes its accessibility workshop series with today's capstone webinar, and Ed-Fi's API v8.0 keeps the data-interoperability layer moving.

**4. AI, Fraud & Research Integrity.** AI-generated **"ghost students"** are defrauding financial-aid systems while AI-assisted grant writing wins more NIH funding — raising questions about what the shortcut costs science.
