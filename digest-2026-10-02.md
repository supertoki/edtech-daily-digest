## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Hype vs. Evidence.** Usage data and efficacy questions show **AI adoption outrunning proof**; Brookings says research must keep pace with tools already used by most teachers.

**2. Security, Risk and Protection.** From Brookings' warnings to EdTech Magazine's **cyber-risk-as-financial-exposure** and shadow-IoT pieces, risk management is moving into the center of AI and tech planning.

**3. Educator Readiness.** Teachers are adopting AI fast, but **training and guidance lag**; relationships and implementation support still decide whether tech works.

**4. Infrastructure, Cost and Standards.** **Hybrid cloud, volatile AI pricing, help-desk automation** and Ed-Fi/1EdTech standards show infrastructure choices shaping what schools can sustain.

**5. AI Tools and Literacy in Classrooms.** Google's new education AI tools and college AI-literacy efforts show **vendors and institutions racing to define classroom AI use**.

**6. Convenings and Community.** **EdTech Week, Tech Congress and ISTE+ASCD** gatherings in October will set the fall agenda.
