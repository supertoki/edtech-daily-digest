# EdTech Daily Digest — September 30, 2026

## Executive Summary

Inside Higher Ed reports new usage data showing **AI integration in college classrooms is overhyped**: the most widely adopted dedicated AI tool, Google Gemini, reached only about 88,000 users across 211 institutions and did not crack the top 100 ed-tech tools. EdSurge's coverage of a new study finds curriculum and edtech providers are **bolting AI onto materials with little evidence it improves learning**, and EdTech Digest argues AI will work best for the students who need it least. Meanwhile, OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is the first model rated **"Critical"** for cyber capability, Brookings' task force says AI's risks currently outweigh its benefits for students, and EdTech Week (Oct 14-15, New York) is set to center on higher-ed reinvention and the AI-native talent stack.

## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Hype vs. Evidence.** Usage data and new studies suggest **AI adoption claims outrun proof** of learning impact in both K-12 and higher ed.

**2. Equity and Student Risk.** Brookings and EdTech Digest warn AI **benefits accrue to advantaged students** while risks to learning capacity fall on others.

**3. Cybersecurity Frontier.** GPT-6 Astra's **"Critical"** cyber rating raises the stakes for school IT security planning.

**4. Data and Standards Infrastructure.** Ed-Fi data standards and 1EdTech interoperability work keep **powering real-time student support** and credential portability.

**5. Educator Learning and PD.** From TikTok-style micro-PD to ISTE+ASCD's six-million-educator AI literacy push, **professional learning is being reinvented**.

**6. Events and Market Signals.** EdTech Week (**Oct 14-15**) and the EdTech Awards highlight where investors and vendors are focusing next.
