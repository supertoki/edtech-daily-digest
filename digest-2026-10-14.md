# EdTech Daily Digest — October 14, 2026

**Governance and evidence dominate.** EdSurge reports an EdReports finding that only one of 10 vendors offered third-party proof their AI features improve learning, and that nearly 30% of K-12 AI policies restrict or prohibit use.
