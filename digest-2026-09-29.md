## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Adoption Outruns the Evidence.** Microsoft finds **~9 in 10** education users have tried AI, while Inside Higher Ed reports the research on AI's learning benefits is **"nonexistent"** and Brookings' task force says risks currently outweigh benefits.

**2. Cyber Risk Keeps Escalating.** GPT-6 Astra's **"Critical"** cyber rating and the Rubrik-CrowdStrike identity-recovery integration underline growing pressure on school and campus security.

**3. Standards and Platforms Get Easier to Plug Into.** Learning Commons opens **state standards and teaching methods** to edtech builders, while AI-driven admissions tooling spreads across 100+ institutions.

**4. Skills and the Entry-Level Squeeze.** **77%** of graduates say employers expect too much experience, even as cyber ranges, school-data career pathways and workplace-learning investment try to bridge the gap.

**5. Screen-Time and Student-AI Guardrails.** CoSN issues new screen-time guidance as **New York and Los Angeles** pause student-facing AI, signaling a more cautious policy turn.

**6. Student-Facing Tech, Broadly Defined.** Accessible classroom audio, VR STEM, and a new K-5 writing program point to product work centered on how students actually experience learning.
