## Key Themes & Trends

**1. AI Value & Evidence.** Brookings' task force and its audit-first guidance keep pressing for **proof before scale**, including a field experiment where unrestricted ChatGPT use lowered later test scores.

**2. AI Policy & Backlash.** **NYC's one-year pause and LA's device removal** mark a turn toward restriction, even as most districts now have AI guidelines.

**3. Student Protection & Trust.** Reports flag that **campus AI adoption outpaces student safeguards**, and students fear false AI-cheating accusations.

**4. Teachers, Faculty & Students.** Teacher readiness lags: a third of teachers lack formal AI guidance, while **free ISTE+ASCD/Google training** targets six million educators.

**5. Standards & Interoperability.** **1EdTech restructures** into a nonprofit plus consortium and Ed-Fi hubs show real-time data at statewide scale.

**6. Fall Events.** **EdTech Week** runs Oct 14–15 at Columbia, and ISTE's GenerationAI cohort convenes Oct 19–20.
